## Purpose

You are invited to test a student-built appointment system and rate how easy it is to use. This is not a real medical service. Your feedback helps improve the interface and the final report.

## What you will do

- Use a demo account to search for a provider, book or manage an appointment, and look around the dashboard.
- Think aloud while you work.
- Answer five rating questions and three short debrief questions.
- Expected time: 30–40 minutes.

## Data and privacy

- Do not enter real medical information, real addresses, or real identification numbers.
- Your name may be recorded as a first name or initials only. The record will show your role group (patient, doctor/clinic, mixed) and your scores.
- Screenshots are of the application only, not of your face, unless you give separate permission below.
- You may stop at any time and ask for your data to be deleted.
- Records are kept in the project documentation and are not published publicly.

## Risks

Minimal. You may find some screens confusing; that is the point of the test. No physical or medical risk is involved.
